# Asset Attribution

All art, audio, and texture assets in SUNBREAK are **CC0 / public domain** (or otherwise free
and redistributable), per the project Cost Rule. Even CC0 assets are credited here as good
practice.

## Approved CC0 sources

- **Kenney** — https://kenney.nl (models, UI, audio)
- **Quaternius** — https://quaternius.com (characters, vehicles, props)
- **Poly Pizza** — https://poly.pizza (low-poly models)
- **Poly Haven** — https://polyhaven.com (HDRIs, PBR textures)
- **ambientCG** — https://ambientcg.com (PBR textures)
- **Mixamo** — https://mixamo.com (rigged characters + animations, free)
- **Freesound** — https://freesound.org (filter to CC0)
- **Sketchfab** — https://sketchfab.com (filter to CC0)

> Pipeline rule: compress every glTF (DRACO/meshopt) and every texture (KTX2/basis) before it
> enters `assets/`, and add a row above with author + license + URL.
